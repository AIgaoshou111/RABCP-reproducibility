The error in your nginx log shows the actual problem:

```
upstream: "http://172.22.0.4:80/favicon.ico"
```

Nginx is trying to connect to port **80** on the wiki container, but your wiki container is listening on port **3000** (as shown in your docker ps output: `0.0.0.0:3000->3000/tcp`).

## The Fix

Your `proxy_pass` directive needs to specify port 3000:

```nginx
proxy_pass http://wiki:3000;
```

## Why is it forwarding to port 80?

When you use `proxy_pass http://wiki;` without specifying a port, nginx defaults to port 80. The `wiki` hostname resolves correctly to the container's IP (172.22.0.4), but nginx tries port 80 instead of port 3000 where the wiki application is actually listening.
